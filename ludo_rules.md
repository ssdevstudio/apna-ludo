# Ludo Official BSE Rules

This document outlines the official rules implemented in this Ludo Game, adhering to the "Ludo Official BSE Rules" document.

## 1. Time Limit & Tie Breaker
* **Game Duration:** The time limit for the game is 45 minutes.
* **Timeout Win Condition:** If a game hasn't been won within the 45-minute limit, the side/player with the least number of active pieces left on the board (i.e. not yet in Home) will win.
* **Tie-Breaker:** In case of a tie at the 45-minute mark, play will continue. The first side/player to reach the "Home" square with any token wins immediately.

## 2. Starting & Basic Movement
* **Entering the Track:** The only way for a player to move a piece from the starting circle (yard) onto the track is by throwing a "6".
* **Options on a 6:** Whenever a "6" is thrown, the player has the option of moving a piece from the yard to the start square, OR moving a piece already on the track by 6 spaces.
* **Extra Turn:** Rolling a "6" grants the player an extra turn. A double "6" (two consecutive sixes) can be used to start two pieces on the track.
* **Triple 6 Penalty:** Rolling three consecutive "6"s (Triple "6") will nullify the throws (end the player's turn immediately) and pass the turn to the next player.

## 3. Capturing
* **Capturing Single Pieces:** If a piece lands on an opponent's single piece on a non-safe square, the piece jumped upon is captured and returned to its starting circle (yard).
* **Safe Squares:** A piece may not be captured if it is on a "Star" square or a "Start" square (safe square). Safe squares NEVER form blockades; opponents can freely pass through or land on safe squares, and multiple pieces on a safe square move individually without blob restrictions.
* **Blob Captures:** A moving Blob (on a non-safe track square) captures another opponent's Blob or single piece if it lands exactly on it.

## 4. Blobs & Blockades (Double Goti on Track)
* **Definition:** When two or more pieces of the same color occupy the same **non-safe** track square, they form a barrier known as a 'Blob' (Blockade).
* **Blocking Opponents:** Single opponent pieces cannot pass through an active Blob in a single move. However, an opponent single piece CAN land exactly on the Blob (coexisting safely with it without capture). On subsequent turns, it may move on and pass it. A moving Blob can pass through or capture an opponent's Blob.
* **Moving a Blob (Even Numbers):** A Blob on a non-safe track square moves together as a unit by **half the dice value** when an **even number** is rolled (e.g. roll 2 -> moves 1 space; roll 4 -> moves 2 spaces; roll 6 -> moves 3 spaces).
* **Breaking a Blob (Odd Numbers):** A Blob cannot be moved together with an odd number (1, 3, 5). When an odd number is rolled, a single piece is moved independently by the full dice roll, breaking the double goti.
* **Safe Squares Exemption:** Multiple tokens sitting on a safe square (Start square or Star square) do NOT form a Blob. They move individually by the full dice roll (whether even or odd) and never block opponent passage.

## 5. Turn Timers & Player Lives
* **Turn Duration:** Each player is given a set time limit (typically 10-30 seconds depending on game configuration) to complete their roll and token movement during their turn.
* **Player Lives:** Each player starts a match with a total of 5 lives (represented by dots below their profile).
* **Life Loss on Timeout:** If a player fails to take an action before their turn timer expires, their turn is automatically skipped and they lose 1 life.
* **No Life Recovery:** A lost life is permanently consumed for that specific match and CANNOT be regained even if the player resumes taking actions in subsequent turns.
* **Elimination:** Once a player loses all 5 lives in a single match, they are eliminated (Timed Out), their yard icon turns black with an "OUT" banner, and any remaining active players continue until a winner is declared (or if only 1 active player remains, they win automatically).
